# Coach marketplace legal review draft

This is product copy for review, not legal, tax, medical, or payment advice. Before public launch, have a qualified attorney and tax professional approve the final language for the jurisdictions where Runnit and its coaches operate.

## Required user-facing disclosures

- Runnit provides the marketplace and payment infrastructure; the coach is the service provider.
- Coaching and training content are not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Athletes should consult a qualified healthcare professional before changing training.
- Coaches publish their own pricing, availability, service description, and cancellation policy.
- A booking is confirmed only after successful payment. Stripe provides the payment receipt; Runnit does not hold coach payout funds outside Stripe Connect's platform rules.
- Athletes can request cancellation, refunds follow the displayed policy, and disputes are handled through the applicable payment and marketplace process.
- Coaches are responsible for their credentials, services, taxes, and compliance obligations. Runnit should obtain required tax forms and issue any legally required reporting.
- Athletes choose whether training data is shared with a coach. Private means Runnit does not disclose training data to the coach through marketplace access.

## Approval checklist

- Marketplace terms and coach agreement
- Commission and Stripe Connect agreement
- Refund, cancellation, and dispute policy
- Privacy/data-sharing consent and retention policy
- Medical disclaimer and coach responsibility language
- Tax forms, receipts, and reporting treatment
- Coach verification standard and appeal process
